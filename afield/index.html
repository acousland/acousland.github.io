Afield

A little further from the everyday.

Afield is a motorcycle journey through rural Mongolia. Ride quiet tracks, walk through open landscapes, and set up camp as the light changes. Its developing story follows a return to a family radio station with a field recorder.

What you can do

01

Follow the valley

Explore an original valley and a separate Kharkhorin / Orkhon Valley map, with tracks, river crossings, and changing daylight.

02

Travel at your own pace

Ride a classic motorcycle, walk independently, park, push, and recover the bike. Choose a first-person cockpit or chase camera.

03

Make a place to pause

Carry and place a tent, chair, and fire, sleep until morning, and save your outing. Story interactions are still in development.

Getting started

  1. Download the Apple silicon Mac disk image and drag Afield into Applications.
  2. Start an outing, choose a landscape, and use the controls guide to explore on foot or mount the motorcycle.
  3. Use the pause menu for settings and saved outings. Check for new versions through Afield → Check for Updates…

A journey still taking shape

The public build is a playable development preview. Exploration, riding, camping, and saved outings are available; the full narrative and story interactions remain in development.

Mac releases are signed and notarised and include automatic updates. The game’s source development stays private.
