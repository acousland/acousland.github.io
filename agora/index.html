Agora

Your word, kept.

Agora is a personal CRM for the people in your life and the promises you make to them. Keep contact notes, conversations, and commitments together in a native Mac app with a local library.

What you can do

01

Remember the person

Keep contact details, tags, and personal notes beside a timeline of emails, meetings, calls, and conversations.

02

Keep track of both sides

Record promises you made and promises made to you, with deadlines, priorities, and the conversation they came from.

03

Bring the context together

See overdue and upcoming commitments in Overview. Save selected Outlook emails or connect an AI client through the local helper.

Getting started

  1. Download the disk image and drag Agora into Applications. The current release is Developer ID signed but not notarised.
  2. Add a person, log a conversation, and record a commitment with its deadline and direction.
  3. Open Connections for Outlook setup, AI-client integration, JSON import/export, and library backups.

A library on your Mac

People, conversation notes, and saved emails are stored in a local library. Agora has no cloud CRM service or analytics.

Outlook captures only emails you explicitly save. The add-in needs a supported mailbox and permission to install custom add-ins; live mailbox capture has not yet been verified. An AI client may share accessed records according to its own settings.
