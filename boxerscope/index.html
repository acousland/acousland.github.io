BoxerScope

Get to know your boxer.

BoxerScope is a native diagnostic app for BMW R1200GS K25 motorcycles using a UniCarScan UCSI-2100 adapter. Read vehicle systems, inspect fault memories, and keep a local history for each bike.

What you can do

01

A garage with a memory

Recognise a bike from its ECU-supplied identifier and keep profiles, scan results, and diagnostic history together.

02

See what the bike reports

Discover supported systems, read available fault memories, and inspect decoded live data alongside retained raw responses.

03

Know the limits

Service and output-test controls show their prerequisites and remain locked until individually verified. Automatic scans are read-only.

Getting started

  1. Download the Mac disk image and drag BoxerScope into Applications. The installer is universal, Developer ID signed, and notarised by Apple.
  2. Pair the UniCarScan UCSI-2100 in macOS System Settings. Connect with the bike’s ignition on and check the recognised identity.
  3. Run a scan, inspect supported live data, and export a diagnostic report for your records.

Built around the real bike

Profiles and diagnostic history stay local. Readings come from the connected adapter and vehicle; the app does not invent vehicle data.

Coverage depends on the exact controller and validated response. Confirm the recognised identifier against the bike, and confirm official service-campaign status with BMW or an authorised repairer.

The public download is for macOS 14 or later on Intel and Apple silicon. iPhone and iPad versions are not currently distributed through these releases.
