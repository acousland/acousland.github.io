See your writing with fresh eyes.

Select a passage and ask co-written to take a look. A quiet menu-bar companion shows useful cues about your writing, with a full window for closer reading and optional AI feedback.

What you can do

01

Understand the voice

Explore point of view, possible passive constructions, formality, tone, and reading ease, with supporting evidence.

02

Find the rhythm

Compare sentence lengths, repeated vocabulary, hedging, and wordy phrasing. Use the cues to decide what to revise.

03

Go a little deeper

Optional AI feedback adds context and practical edits. Style-pattern reviews offer explanations rather than an authorship verdict.

Getting started

  1. Download the disk image and drag co-written into Applications. It starts in the menu bar.
  2. Grant Accessibility in Settings, select some text, and press ⇧⌘L. You can also paste a passage.
  3. Read the dropdown, or open the full app to explore the evidence. Add your own API key if you want AI feedback.

You choose what to share

Local analysis works on your Mac without saving a writing history. The menu-bar shortcut analyses the passage you request; the full window can follow settled selections while it is open.

AI analysis shares the requested passage with your chosen destination and uses your own key. Local style cues are mainly for English; readability and style scores are estimates.
