Mondrian

Give your ideas a shape.

Mondrian turns structured diagram data into a visual workspace. Organise drawings in a local library, refine their layout and appearance, and work with an AI client through the bundled local helper.

What you can do

01

A library for your thinking

Keep drawings in folders and separate libraries. Search by name, author, tags, and drawing details.

02

Make the picture your own

Work with shapes, connectors, custom stencils, layout controls, and saved versions. Inspect details and revisit the history of a drawing.

03

From ideas to something shareable

Let a connected AI client create or update drawings, then export the result as PNG, PDF, SVG, HTML, or JSON.

Getting started

  1. Download the disk image, open it, and drag Mondrian into Applications.
  2. Create a drawing or import diagram JSON, then adjust its shapes, connectors, and layout.
  3. Use Settings → AI Clients to connect a supported client, or work directly in the app.

Your library stays with you

Drawings, history, stencils, and attachments live in local libraries. The app works without an account or internet connection.

A connected AI client can read and change drawings through the local helper. Any sharing with a model provider follows that client’s settings.
