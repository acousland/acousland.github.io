OSIntel

Follow the evidence.

OSIntel is a native Mac app for corporate intelligence and technology due diligence. Keep research about an organisation in one workspace, with public documents, observed technology, source links, and a record of how the evidence was gathered.

What you can do

01

Build a research case

Gather public company information, documents, and technology evidence around the organisation you are investigating.

02

Keep the source in view

Explore findings, relationships, and a timeline with provenance, so a briefing stays connected to its supporting material.

03

Turn research into a briefing

Use optional AI-assisted synthesis and questions over collected evidence, then export findings for further work.

Getting started

  1. Download the public Mac installer from Releases and move OSIntel into Applications.
  2. Follow the setup guide for any lookup credentials and your own AI API key.
  3. Create an investigation, gather evidence, and review the linked sources before preparing a briefing.

An evolving research workspace

The newer local workspace focuses on document-led research. The current public download is an earlier release and may not include every feature under development.

Credentials are stored in Keychain. AI-assisted research can share selected investigation material with the provider; review the app’s sharing prompt. Active reconnaissance requires authorisation for the target.
